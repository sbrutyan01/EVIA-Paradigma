## Middle mouse button moves the canvas

Hold the middle button (the wheel press) over a diagram and drag: the canvas follows the mouse. It works on empty space
and on a block, in the main program and in a class window, at every zoom. Blocks are not moved or selected by it, and
the saved file does not change. The new entry is in the guide's shortcut table (English, Russian, Armenian).

The check `scripts/check-zoom.sh` now also drags with the middle button on the canvas and on a block and compares the
view position.
